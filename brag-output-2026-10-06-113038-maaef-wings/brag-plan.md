# Maaef — the four wings (group video)

A short summary of what Maaef's four wings do, for **Maaef as a whole**, not Maaef Media. The HUD reads "Maaef · The Sovereign Collective", and the close is the Maaef mark + MAAEF wordmark, not the MEDIA lockup.

**Sources**
- Wing content: *Maaef Incorporated — The Collective* (the four wings, their one-liners, services and "worth saying" points; Choose your crew; the loop; "The more the merrier. We're all about M.").
- Group framing: *Maaef Brand Bible* ("a Lucknow-based group of four distinct but connected businesses").
- Look: *Maaef Media House Brand Guidelines*. Panchang SemiBold headers, Verdana Bold sub-headers, Verdana Regular body; white / black / dark maroon #7a0e0e / light grey #e0e0e0 / grey #747375 only; the mark used as supplied (no glow, effects or rotation); the Maaef Star (*) as an accent.
- Pronunciation: Maaef = "Maa-Yuf". The voice script spells it "Maayuf" (espeak phonemes mˈɑːjʌf).

## Sound
- The funk groove (120 BPM, Dm7 | G9), arranged for 75s: an intro, the groove, a riser into the wings, the full band through the wings, a half-time breakdown for the crew, then back up into the close with a final chord.
- **SFX:** only soft swishes under the scene changes and the riser into the wings. Nothing fires on individual words or letters (no zips, slashes, blips or typing ticks).
- **Voice:** Kokoro `af_heart` with the energetic treatment (exclamation phrasing, +4% pitch lift, presence EQ and compression) at a relaxed pace; the music ducks 9 dB under it.
