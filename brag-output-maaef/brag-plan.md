# Maaef.com — brag plan

**What it is:** maaef.com, rebuilt in React/Next.js (the `Maaef-Shift-To-React` repo). It's the home of Maaef Group, a Lucknow media house and business collective.
**Who it's for:** Founders, SMEs and brands who need to be looked at: restaurants, real estate, government and high-growth founders.
**What sets it apart:** The site is itself the pitch. It has a cinematic broadcast HUD, an F1 trailer intro, scramble text, a glowing wordmark and real work reels.
**Funniest real claim:** "A media house for people with short attention spans." The video itself respects that.
**Visual hook:** "We make things worth looking at." slamming in word by word over the F1 trailer, inside the live-broadcast HUD.
**Real UI/material shown:** hero copy and HUD, "WE ENGINEER ATTENTION", the Ideas/Executions/Results section, the Video/Web/Design/Photo/Brand strips, the real work reels (F1, Lucknow/district one, Harvest with me, chat ad, Drakkar), the four arms, "That's what we are", the N mark and the glowing Maaef wordmark.
**Tone:** cinematic, trailer-scale; black #050505, red #7a0e0e, Panchang + Courier-style mono HUD.
**Share caption:** We make things worth looking at. The new maaef.com is live.
