# Maaef — The Sovereign Collective: funky cut

Restyled after the reference video ("Motion graphics?"): a dark blueprint grid with a moving 3D floor and camera drift, glowing display type that lights up letter by letter, typed mono lines with a cursor, a connected box diagram with glowing dots travelling along the wires, a slash through words, a white paper "FORM" break with hand-drawn marks, whip transitions with motion blur, and a HUD (fig. labels, timecode, ruler).

**Source rule (unchanged):** all copy comes from the collective PDF. The visual identity is the Maaef brand kit: the mark, Panchang, mono, and brand red #7a0e0e (used as a glow, highlight block and lines) on black/white. The HUD labels ("fig. 0X — …", "form — …") only name the PDF's own sections.

## Sound
An original funk groove at 120 BPM, a D Dorian vamp (Dm7 | G9). It has a syncopated kick, snare + clap on 2 and 4, 16th hats, a slap-bass riff, wah clav stabs and brass hits on the big moments. It breaks down for the paper form and lands on a Dm9 chord. SFX are typing ticks, clav blips on the light-ups, tonal whips on the transitions and ticks on the checkboxes, all in key.
