# LVII Co-living — brag plan

**What it is:** A waitlist site for LVII Co-living: premium flats in Lucknow, renovated and run for you. Meals, cleaning, laundry, staff and repairs are included. You pay rent plus your share of electricity.
**Who it's for:** Young working professionals in Lucknow who end the day doing a "second shift" of chores.
**What sets it apart:** One bill, zero chores. The house runs itself.
**Funniest real claim:** "One bill. Zero chores. That's it, that's the post."
**Visual hook:** 9:47 pm, with the house texting you (Didi, Flat 4B, Landlord, Mummy).
**Real UI shown:** the story's notifications, chore lists, the "Sorted" checklist, the bill card, the pine/LVII intro with bands, and the survey question cards.
**Tone:** `default`, with the site's Gen Z/Hinglish voice. Punchy and warm, on brand (green #065442, deep #032E24, mist, lamp #F2B544; Tinos + Onest).
**Share caption:** Your 9 pm, minus the second shift. LVII Co-living is coming to Lucknow.

## Sound
An original, synthesized upbeat house track at 120 BPM in C major (C–G–Am–F). It has four-on-the-floor kicks, claps, off-beat hats, sidechain-pumped pads, bouncing octave bass and off-beat chord stabs, with a lead hook in the drops. SFX (pops, blips, clicks, whooshes, impacts) are in key and placed on the same beats.
