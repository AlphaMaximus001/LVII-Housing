# Maaef Incorporated — The Sovereign Collective: brag plan

**Source rule:** every word on screen comes from *Maaef Incorporated — The Collective* (PDF). The visual elements come only from the PDF (red #7a0e0e / black / white pages, red rules, black "worth saying" panels, mono captions) and the Maaef brand kit (the Maaef mark, the Panchang display face, the mono face). **What it is:** Maaef, a Lucknow collective of four arms (.enterprises, .studio, .media and .afterhours) that brands can hire one at a time or all together.
**Who it's for:** brands and institutions that need consulting, making, storytelling or hosting.
**What sets it apart:** "A collective that refuses to be one thing." Four currencies, four arms, one loop.
**Hook:** MAAEF assembling letter by letter on the red cover, "The Sovereign Collective".
**Closing line:** "The more the merrier. We're all about M."
**Tone:** polished and confident, with motion graphics; the deck's red/black/white rhythm, beat-timed but restrained.
